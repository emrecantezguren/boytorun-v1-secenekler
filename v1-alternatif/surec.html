Process

From discovery to handover: one partner, one schedule, one budget.

How we work

Six steps to a space ready for work.

OfficeMercedes-Benz Headquarters

  1. Discovery and brief

    We listen to your team, the way you work and your goals, and survey the space on site.

  2. Concept and layout

    We review layout scenarios and the design concept together.

  3. Design and budget

    We develop the design in detail and finalise materials and budget.

  4. Tender and procurement

    We manage cost estimates, specifications and supplier selection.

  5. Construction and supervision

    We control quality, schedule and budget on site.

  6. Handover

    We hand over a space ready to use, and stay by your side afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

What services do you offer?

Pre-design consultancy, architecture and interior design, project and construction management, tender services, engineering coordination and turnkey delivery.

Do you design only, or build as well?

Both. We can design the project, supervise its construction or deliver it turnkey.

What size of projects do you take on?

From headquarters to a single showroom; from small, detailed works to large-scale projects.

Do you work outside Istanbul?

Yes. We have projects in cities across Türkiye and abroad.

What do you need to prepare a proposal?

Project type, approximate area and city are enough; drawings or photos speed things up. We reply within one business day.

Let’s plan your next space.

Tell us the project type, approximate area and city. We will take it from there.
